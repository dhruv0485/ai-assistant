Responsibilities

Grow Your Skills, Grow Your Potential

Do you want to be a part of a tight knit team, who directly impacts the customer experience for a Fortune 50 company? Do you want to be a part of a fast paced, constantly changing environment where passion for how we communicate with our customers is a must? Come join the Life Communications product team at State Farm!

The Life Communications product team is tasked with modernizing the way we communicate with Life Policyholders, through a digital first omnichannel strategy. This team is currently undergoing several modernization projects including a new AWS hosted front end User Initiated Communication tool, the Life Modernization (API, Schemas, LPAT) development work and a transition from antiquated technologies to a more modern solution for populating legacy policy pages. The selected applicant will join the team as a Software Engineer for the Oracle Insurance Policy Administration (OIPA) system and Equisoft Design manager. This team uses the OIPA Platform as its policy administration system for all Life products at State Farm.

This individual should be dedicated to delivering high quality results, digging in, triaging problems and showing that continuous growth and improvement mindset. The Software Engineer should be able to establish a vision that goes beyond the here and now and provides a pathway into the future.
