Job Description:

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will focus on engineering user interfaces and interactions. You’ll play a crucial role in iterating and continuously deploying new features, functionality, and products to our customers. You’ll collaborate closely with backend and product teams to build optimized experiences for our end-users within a lean DevOps and automation culture.

Key Responsibilities:

Design and architect cloud-native and service-oriented UI components and applications.

Collaborate with team members to design complex applications and suggest optimizations.

Develop creative solutions for high-performing web applications.

Influence product teams to maintain design system standards and adoption.

Write clean, valuable code daily to add new features and capabilities.

Investigate and resolve production issues across UI and service layers.

Qualifications:

5-10 years of experience in designing and architecting scalable web applications.

Proficiency in ES6+ JavaScript/TypeScript and modern reactive JavaScript libraries.

Experience In a Startup Environment Preferred.

Strong collaboration and communication skills.

Commitment to agile methodology and lean principles.
